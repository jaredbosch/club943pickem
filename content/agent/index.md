# thepickempool — NFL Pick'em Pools with Live Spreads & Confidence Scoring

thepickempool.com is a free, private NFL pick'em pool platform built for ATS (against the spread) confidence scoring. It hosts private leagues for groups of 8–100+ players, with live market spreads sourced from DraftKings, FanDuel, and BetMGM, real commissioner tools, and a real-time picks matrix called The Grid. It is not a public contest — it is a platform for running your own private league with friends, family, or coworkers.

## What it does

- Hosts private NFL pick'em pools (create a league in about 30 seconds, invite with a code)
- Live ATS spreads that update throughout the week and lock at each game's individual kickoff time — not all at once on Thursday
- Six pool formats: ATS + Confidence, Straight Up + Confidence, ATS Only, Straight Up Only, Pick 5 ATS, Pick 5 Straight Up
- Confidence scoring: assign unique point values (1 to the number of games) to each pick; correct picks earn those points, wrong picks earn zero
- Commissioner tools: payment tracking, player management, registration lock, invite codes
- The Grid: every player's picks and confidence numbers side by side, updated live as games kick off
- Player analytics: confidence calibration, performance by line size, primetime splits, streaks
- Free to use, no ads inside the app
