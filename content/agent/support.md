# thepickempool Help & Support

## Common questions

**How do I create a league?**
Sign up free at [thepickempool.com](https://thepickempool.com), choose a format, and share your league's invite code. Setup takes about 30 seconds.

**How do I join a league?**
Get the invite code from your commissioner, create a free account, and enter the code.

**When do picks lock?**
In full-slate formats, each pick locks at that game's kickoff time. In Pick 5 formats, all picks lock Friday.

**How does confidence scoring work?**
You assign each pick a unique point value from 1 up to the number of games that week. Correct picks earn those points; wrong picks earn zero. See [How NFL Confidence Pools Work](https://thepickempool.com/blog/how-nfl-confidence-pool-works).

**Where do the spreads come from?**
Live market lines from DraftKings, FanDuel, and BetMGM. They update through the week and freeze for you at kickoff.

**How do tiebreakers work?**
Weekly ties are broken by closest guess on the Monday Night Football combined score. See [NFL Pick'em Pool Tiebreaker Rules](https://thepickempool.com/blog/nfl-pickem-pool-tiebreaker-rules).

**Is it really free?**
Yes — free to create a league, free to join, no ads inside the app.
